## 2. Governing statistical contract

Keep two fields and three attack seeds inside a person cluster; seeds are not independent new persons. - Nondegenerate cell: person-bootstrap 95% percentile interval.
- 0/n or n/n cell: Wilson on `n_eff = 2*n_persons/DEFF`, DEFF=1.5 at assumed ICC≈0.5; between-arm contrast Newcombe/MOVER from two Wilson intervals. Text uses the single-seed formula here while other passages discuss m=6, DEFF=3.5 at three seeds.
